The JMS project, aka "Je me souviens", is a game project. It aims to be a tactical roleplaying game set in a post-apocalyptic future in the [Montréal underground city](https://www.mtl.org/en/experience/guide-underground-city-shopping) and the Métro de Montréal network. Disconnected from the surface, the residents must make do with what can be scavenged from the numerous malls and facilities, though mysterious forces and strange creatures make travel outside of settlements unreliable and unsafe. 

The project leverages the Bevy engine.

The gameplay is turn-based, tile-based, for discrete actions with measurable results, run at whatever pace the player desires. It aims to be a bit of a compromise between a traditional roguelike and a CRPG.

- Short character life span (permadeath), but future characters keep working within the same world
- Pacing control, actions are quick and simple, but complex challenges may use as much time as desired to solve
- Low narrative exposition (cinematics, forced events, etc)
- Turns are simultaneous for all entities and represent about 1 second worth of actions
